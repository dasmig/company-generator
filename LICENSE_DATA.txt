company-generator data (resources/) — sources and licences
==========================================================

The code is under the MIT licence (LICENSE.MIT). The tables in resources/ are
derived from the sources below; only counts, shares and word statistics are
kept from them, never a firm's name or number. See doc/data.md for what each
source is used for.

Eurostat (structural business statistics, business demography, legal form,
labour force survey) — © European Union, reuse authorised under the Eurostat
reuse policy (Commission Decision 2011/833/EU), source acknowledged.

OECD Structural and Demographic Business Statistics — © OECD, CC BY 4.0.

ILOSTAT (International Labour Organization) — CC BY 4.0.

US Census Bureau (Business Dynamics Statistics, Nonemployer Statistics) and
the IRS Statistics of Income — US Government works, public domain.

GLEIF (Entity Legal Forms code list, ISO 20275; LEI golden copy) — CC0 1.0.

Companies House, Basic Company Data — contains public sector information
licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

INSEE, base SIRENE des entreprises et de leurs établissements — Licence
Ouverte / Etalab 2.0.

Brønnøysund Register Centre, Enhetsregisteret — Norwegian Licence for Open
Government Data (NLOD) 2.0.

National statistics offices' published business counts (China NBS, India
Ministry of MSME, Indonesia BPS, Pakistan PBS, Bangladesh BBS, Russia FTS,
Brazil Mapa de Empresas, Mexico INEGI, Japan Statistics Bureau, Vietnam GSO,
Egypt CAPMAS, UK DBT, ABS, Statistics Canada, Statistics Korea) — facts read
from their publications; see scripts/curated/census.tsv for each.

Places: address-generator's places, from GeoNames — CC BY 4.0.

Surnames: name-generator (dasmig) — MIT.

Curated tables in scripts/curated/ (word lists, legal-form templates and
overrides, name patterns, TLD shares, number schemes) — written for this
library, MIT.
